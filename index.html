01 / INTRODUCTION

About me.

I am an Electrical and Computer Engineer, researcher, and educator with a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from The University of Alabama, where I serve as an Assistant Teaching Professor.

My work combines radio-frequency (RF) engineering, radar systems, digital signal processing, embedded systems, and FPGA/RFSoC hardware development. During my doctoral research, I developed compact UAV-based ultra-wideband radar technologies for measuring snow depth, characterizing dielectric properties, and estimating snow water equivalent.

I'm particularly interested in turning advanced sensing research into practical, reliable systems — from wireless and Wi-Fi-based sensing to microwave and millimeter-wave radar. Beyond research, I enjoy teaching, technical problem-solving, and connecting engineering innovation with real-world needs.
