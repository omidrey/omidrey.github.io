01 / ABOUT

Research-driven.
Application-focused.

My work spans radar instrumentation, RF and microwave systems, digital signal processing, FPGA-based data acquisition, and embedded computing.

I earned my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from The University of Alabama, where my doctoral research focused on small-uncrewed-aircraft-based ultra-wideband radar for snow characterization, including snow depth, dielectric permittivity, and snow water equivalent estimation.

Alongside research, I teach and develop courses in digital logic, microcomputers, embedded systems, computer architecture, electronics, and signals and systems.
